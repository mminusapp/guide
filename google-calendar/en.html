Google Calendar Setup Guide

Overview

M-Cal uses iOS EventKit, so once you add your Google account in iPhone Settings, your Google Calendar events will automatically appear in M-Cal.

Setup Steps

  1. Open the Settings app on your iPhone.
  2. Tap Apps.
  3. Tap Calendar.
  4. Tap Calendar Accounts.
  5. Tap Add Account, then select Google and sign in. If your Google account is already added, tap the account name instead.
  6. Make sure Calendars is turned on.

That's it. Open M-Cal and your Google Calendar events will be displayed.

Holiday Calendars

You can add holiday calendars for your region through Google Calendar.

  1. Open Google Calendar in your browser.
  2. Click the + next to "Other calendars" on the left side.
  3. Select Browse calendars of interest.
  4. Under Regional holidays, check the countries you want to display.

The holiday calendars you add will automatically sync to M-Cal through your iPhone.

Troubleshooting

Events not showing up

Go to Settings > Apps > Calendar > Calendar Accounts and verify that Calendars is enabled for your Google account.

Sync takes a while

Syncing with Google Calendar may take a few minutes. Pull down on the calendar screen in M-Cal to refresh.
