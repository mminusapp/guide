Getting started

Open Settings with the gear icon on the calendar screen. On iPhone, tap the gear at the top of the screen. On Mac, click the gear at the top left.

All settings are stored only on your device. Nothing is sent anywhere.

Calendar

Calendar access

M-Cal shows the calendars on this device. If your events don't appear, allow calendar access. When access isn't allowed, the Settings screen shows an Allow or Open Settings button.

Add Calendar Account

This is the entry point for adding other calendar accounts, such as Google or iCloud. On iPhone it opens the Calendar accounts in the Settings app; on Mac it opens Internet Accounts in System Settings. Events from added accounts appear in M-Cal automatically.

Google Calendar guideiPhone only

Step-by-step instructions for connecting Google Calendar are in a separate guide.

Open the Google Calendar guide

Share Important Marks

In the calendar list, press and hold a calendar (right-click on Mac) and choose it. In a calendar where it's on, Important marks set by anyone appear on your important bar and as stars. Removing a mark removes it for everyone. Use it for a calendar like your children's, where you both want to share each other's Important marks. Turn it on for the same calendar on the other person's device too. Calendars where it's on show a star next to the name.

Language

Language

Choose the language M-Cal uses. It changes right away.

Display

Time Format

Choose whether times are shown in 12-hour (AM/PM) or 24-hour format.

Start of Week

Choose the first day of the week: Monday or Sunday. (When the app language is Arabic, Saturday is also available.)

Show Hijri DatesOnly when the app language is Arabic

Shows the Hijri (Islamic) date together with each date.

Show RokuyoOnly when the app language is Japanese

Shows the Rokuyo, the traditional Japanese six-day cycle (Senshō, Tomobiki, Senbu, Butsumetsu, Taian, Shakkō), in the month view and elsewhere.

Time Zone SupportMac only

When on, the event editor shows a Time Zone row so you can pick a time zone for each event. It's off by default. iPhone has no such switch; the Time Zone row is always shown.

Show Week Numbers

Shows which week of the year each week is.

Sort by Calendar Order

In the month view, events on each day are ordered by the order of your calendar list: events from calendars higher in the list appear higher. Put your own calendars at the top and calendars shared with you below, and your own events always come first. You can reorder the calendar list by dragging, and newly added calendars go to the bottom. When off, events are ordered by time.

Order Multi-day Bars Too

Available when “Sort by Calendar Order” is on. When on, events from calendars you've placed below your own calendars, including their multi-day bars, appear below your own events, so bars from calendars shared with you no longer sit above your events. Your own calendars are the default calendar (where new events are saved) and the calendars above it in the list. If it looks hard to read, turn it off to go back to the original look, with all bars above the day's events.

Dim Past Events

Fades events that have already passed so upcoming events stand out.

Show Important Bar

Shows your nearest Important event at the top of the screen, together with the days remaining. To mark an event as important, turn on Important in the event editor. Important marks set by other people in shared calendars are not shown; only the ones you set appear.

Appearance

Choose the screen brightness: System (follows your device), Light, or Dark.

Map App

Choose which app opens an event's location: Apple Maps or Google Maps.

Notifications

Permission

Reminders need notification permission. If you haven't allowed it yet, you can do so here. After you've declined once, change it in your device's system settings.

Badge Count

Shows the number of today's events on the app icon. Turning this on may ask for notification permission.

Defaults / New Event

These are the starting values for new events. You can change them for each event when you create it.

Reminder

The starting reminder for new events, from None up to 1 week before.

All-day Reminder

The starting reminder for new all-day events: no reminder, same day (12:00 AM), same day (9:00 AM), 1 day before (9:00 AM), 2 days before (9:00 AM), 1 week before (9:00 AM), or Custom to pick the time on the day yourself.

Event Duration

The starting length of new events, from 15 minutes to 2 hours.

Calendar

The calendar new events are saved to. With Default, they go to the default calendar set in your device's Calendar app.

Time Zone

Time zone for each event

You can pick a time zone for each event. For example, if you add “19:00–23:00 Paris time” while you're in Japan, it appears in the early morning of the next day in Japan time. When you arrive in Paris and your device's time zone changes, it appears at 19:00–23:00.

Shown in event details

The event details show the time in your device's time zone, plus the time in the event's own time zone (for example, Paris 19:00–23:00). Events in the same time zone as your device show nothing extra.

Floating

Floating makes an event without a time zone: it shows the same time wherever you are (19:00 stays 19:00). It suits things like “a 7 a.m. jog every morning”. For events at one specific moment worldwide, like flights and meetings, give them a time zone.

All Day

All-day events aren't affected by time zones. They appear on the same date wherever you are.

Searching by city

In the time zone picker, search by city name. The time zones you picked recently are listed at the top.
